# Loop Archer

Language for combat and permanent progression in the independent Python game,
including the mechanics retained from the original.

## Language

**Attempt**:
One visit to the combat arena, ending in death, retirement or completion.
_Avoid_: Loop, when referring only to one combat attempt

**Loop**:
The repeating cycle of combat, an attempt ending, purchasing permanent upgrades,
and returning to combat.

**Recall**:
Pulling a stopped arrow toward the archer while the recall control is held.

**Encounter**:
A named campaign wave with an enemy composition, tactical goal and spawn rhythm.

**Pack**:
A small group of reinforcements separated from the next group by a longer gap.

**Regroup**:
The two-second break after a cleared wave. The clock and passive income freeze
while movement and arrow retrieval remain available.

**Portal wrap**:
An arrow crossing a horizontal arena boundary and reappearing on the other side
without reversing direction.

**Ricochet**:
An arrow reflecting from a vertical arena boundary and changing direction.

**Skill tree**:
The connected map of permanent upgrades explored between combat attempts.
_Avoid_: Shop grid, when describing the original game's spatial tree

**Rear core**:
The Warden's weak point, behind its committed attack direction.

**Exposure window**:
The brief recovery after a Warden charge when its rear core can take damage.

**Skillshot**:
A kill made with an arrow that used a portal, ricochet or recall.

**Skillshot chain**:
Consecutive skillshot kills close enough together to earn an additional bonus.
An ordinary kill or a long gap ends the chain.

**Rank**:
One purchased level of a permanent upgrade.

**Owned rank**:
The number of ranks permanently purchased, retained when an upgrade is disabled.

**Equipped rank**:
The selected effective rank, from zero to owned rank. Combat snapshots it at the
start of an attempt. The shop discount uses its current equipped rank.

**Loadout**:
The equipped ranks chosen for the next attempt, without removing purchases.

**Multi-kill burst**:
A sequence of kills no more than 0.4 seconds apart, rewarding the second and
subsequent kills independently of skillshot route bonuses.

**Maxed**:
All available ranks have been purchased. Which version the player has maxed must
be stated explicitly when comparing the original and recreation.

**Fully upgraded**:
The permanent skill tree has no remaining ranks to purchase. This does not imply
that the player has completed the finale or mastery challenges.

**Finale**:
The designed concluding encounter of the main progression, distinct from optional
challenges played afterward.

**Mastery challenge**:
An optional objective that tests use of existing combat mechanics after or beyond
the main progression.
